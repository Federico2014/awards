# Code of Conduct

[中文](docs/zh/code-of-conduct.md)

Participants should make this project a respectful place for mathematical discussion, independent verification, and good-faith disagreement.

- Discuss evidence and reasoning. Critique claims without attacking people.
- Welcome different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience.
- Disclose relevant conflicts and respect recusals.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, impersonate others, publish private personal information, or repeatedly disrupt discussions.
- Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may request edits, hide or remove abusive material, lock a discussion, or restrict participation in proportion to the conduct. When possible, explain the action and provide a route for reconsideration by an uninvolved maintainer.

For non-sensitive conduct concerns, flag the relevant public discussion for maintainers without repeating harmful content. A confidential reporting contact and an independent appeal contact are **not yet designated**. Do not publish sensitive reports in issues; those contacts must be established before the program launches. Maintainers with a conflict should not decide the report.
