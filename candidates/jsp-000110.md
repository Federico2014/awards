## Accepted contribution and evidence

The [catalog problem](../problems/catalog-0101-0200.md#JSP-000110) is Erdős 97:
the four-equidistant-vertices question for convex polygons and its general
fixed-k version. The accepted construction gives a negative answer. The separate
extremal-growth question for JSP-000109 / Erdős 96 is outside this notice; see
[the separate scope discussion #4923](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/issues/4923).

- Mathematical evidence: *Unit distances in convex polygons*, Theorem 1.1 and
  Corollary 1.2, [manuscript](https://github.com/lkruer/erdos-96-97-proof/blob/0e98f5f9bdaf36007e3eb405cbefe2eda778a9b2/96-97.pdf)
  and [TeX authorship source](https://github.com/lkruer/erdos-96-97-proof/blob/0e98f5f9bdaf36007e3eb405cbefe2eda778a9b2/96-97.tex).
- Accepted proof version: `lkruer/erdos-96-97-proof`, branch `main`, commit
  `0e98f5f9bdaf36007e3eb405cbefe2eda778a9b2`,
  [Erdos9697Complete.lean](https://github.com/lkruer/erdos-96-97-proof/blob/0e98f5f9bdaf36007e3eb405cbefe2eda778a9b2/Erdos9697Complete.lean).
  The recorded targets are `Proof.erdos_97_false`, `Proof.erdos_97_general_false`
  and `Proof.explicit_erdos_97_counterexample`.
- [The maintainer's verification record](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/pull/289#issuecomment-6463083838)
  reports accepted and solved for `Proof.erdos_97_false`, faithful statement
  correspondence, a passed Lean kernel check and a passed axiom audit using only
  `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. This notice adopts that existing
  record; it does not claim a new build, a separate job for every supporting
  theorem, or an independent human peer review.
