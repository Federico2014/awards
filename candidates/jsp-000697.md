## Accepted scope and fixed evidence

The [catalog problem](../problems/catalog-0601-0700.md#JSP-000697) is Erdős 841.
The accepted formalization answers the original density-zero question: for every
function eta tending to zero, the integers with actual minimum square-completing
delay t(n) at least n^(1-eta(n)) form a set of natural density zero. No sign,
monotonicity or rate assumption on eta is imposed. The later stronger
Dickman-distribution result is outside this candidate's scope.
