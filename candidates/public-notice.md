# Public notice

[Candidates](README.md) · [Award process](../docs/award-process.md)

Mathematical solution and Lean formalization have independent **14-day public
review periods**. Start times below are in UTC; each scheduled period ends
14 full days after its start. A challenge raised during a period must be resolved
before that review can conclude. Written recipient confirmation is required
before a candidate becomes an announced award.

| Problem | Formalization review start (UTC) | Formalization candidate | Solver review start (UTC) | Solver candidate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

No candidates are currently published for public review.

Register the solver candidate first, after mathematical solution review passes.
At this stage the Lean candidate and formalization review date cells stay empty;
the solver's review date also stays empty while formalization is missing.
Only after solver registration and successful Lean verification may maintainers
add the Lean contributor to the same problem row and set the applicable review
start times. Do not register a Lean candidate with no prior solver candidate record.
Existing attribution alone does not establish that registration.

A blank date means that role has not entered public review. In particular, an
accepted solver awaiting formalization has no review start time. At acceptance
and merge, maintainers publish the applicable start time and candidate record
link here; when a previously missing formalization becomes available, they notify
the solver and start the solver's period.

Before identity confirmation, display only the maintainer-assigned recipient
placeholder. After confirmation and consent, use the authorized name. A candidate
cell links to the corresponding contribution record; the problem cell links to
the problem-bank entry. Candidates have not yet received an award.

A successful priority challenge replaces the affected candidate and start time
with those of the replacement's publication. The unaffected role keeps its own
clock. If a contribution is invalidated without an accepted replacement, remove
its candidate/date cells from the active notice; do not show a new review period.
Where no valid formalization remains, remove the affected solver's review dates
as well and retain the solver application awaiting formalization. Keep prior
published dates in the record history. Routine corrections do not reset a clock.
Follow the [challenge process](../docs/award-process.md#the-14-day-public-review)
and submit supporting evidence through the [dispute issue form](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/dispute.yml).
If proposing a replacement proof or catalog update, submit a linked PR using the
[normal submission template](../.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md).
Send private identity evidence only to **thejustinsunprize@hejustinsun.com**.

Detailed challenge handling, identity-verification materials, application timestamps,
payment/delivery information and email correspondence do not belong in this table.
When a role is announced while the other remains a candidate, replace its cell
with an explicitly labeled link to the award record, retaining the completed
review dates in that record. This preserves the prior solver registration link
while Lean review or confirmation is still pending. Remove the problem row once
neither role remains a candidate.
