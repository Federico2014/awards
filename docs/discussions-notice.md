# Discussions notice

[中文](zh/discussions-notice.md)

Discussions is for general questions and conversation. A post or comment here is **not a formal dispute** under Chapter 9 and does not establish that a dispute was filed within the 30-day window.

For a formal objection to an announced award, open an issue using the [dispute form](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/dispute.yml). Recommendations and corrections also use their corresponding issue forms. Accepted and declined submissions must receive a public reason and be archived in the issue thread. Do not publish unconfirmed recipient names or private information.
