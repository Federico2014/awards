# Solution, formalization, and date review · 2026-09-11

[Back to problem index](README.md) · [Individual results for 984 Erdős problems](audit-erdos-20260911.md)

The cutoff is **2026-01-01 onward**, with solution and solution-formalization dates assessed separately. This review compared status sources across the full bank and investigated the 67 Solved/Lean Yes records and five records with pending or conditional formalizations. Lean and Comparator were not rerun locally, and award eligibility was not reassessed.

## Findings

- The solved/open classification of all 984 Erdős problems agrees with the current official database. Its 63 Solved/Lean Yes entries have official solution-formalization records. #390 and #1112 are open + Lean; their conservative Progress/Pending confirmation labels can remain, but must not be interpreted as an absence of formalization.
- **Sendov's complete-solution year was incorrect.** The workbook used a partial 2020 result as the full solution date. This is corrected to 2026, changing elapsed time from 62 to 68 years.
- Sources support the 2026 counterexample and formalization for the Jacobian conjecture in n≥3. The contradictory publication text saying still open and treating 2026 as hypothetical was corrected. Primary sources also support Anderson's 2026 solution and formalization; dates were added and the unverified model attribution corrected.
- **The bank includes results solved before 2026.** The end-of-2025 database already recorded solutions to 215 Erdős problems. Adding Poincaré, moving sofa, Catalan, and modularity gives at least 219 records with pre-2026 solutions. These records are retained.
- Another 99 Erdős problems changed from historically open to solved, while 31 currently solved problems were absent from the historical snapshot. Neither fact alone establishes a first solution in 2026; these dates remain unverified. An open problem's assumed solution date of 2026-09-16 is not a result date.
- External axioms or sorry in early Lean files do not necessarily describe the current proof. Later public versions completing dependencies or providing alternative proofs were found for all 11 historical concerns investigated. Existing Yes values were therefore not downgraded based on old code. Independent replay is still needed before source reports can become local verification findings.
